Authentic Mexican takeout, fresh & made to order.

In its own words: “Family owned authentic Mexican takeout. Fresh food, made to order.” Vazquez is a family-owned takeout on Main Street in Milbridge — fresh homemade tortillas, tacos, tamales and house specialties, made when you order.
